What this measures

A least-squares regression line is fitted to each stock's last 100 bars on the timeframe you pick — daily, weekly or monthly — with a channel drawn 2 standard deviations of the residuals above and below it. Same construction as TradingView's Linear Regression Channel at length 100. Position in channel is where the current price sits between the bands: 0% is the lower band, 100% the upper, and below 0% means price has broken beneath the channel. Trend strength is Pearson R of the fit and swings counts how often price travelled from the top of the channel to the bottom over the window. The newest bar is live, so positions move during the session.

Data

Ten years of split-adjusted daily bars per stock from Yahoo Finance, resampled into weekly and monthly bars — the same aggregation your charting platform uses. Stocks without enough history for a timeframe are left out of it; those under a full 100-bar window are marked short. Spin-offs are the main place data providers disagree on adjusted history, so sanity-check anything surprising against your own chart.
